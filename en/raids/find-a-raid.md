# Find a raid

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/finden.md) · 🇬🇧 English

Here is how to find planned raids. The reply to `/raid list` is **private**—only you can see it.

## Step by step

1. Click the message box at the bottom of Discord.
2. Type `/raid list`.
3. Select **raid list** from the bot's suggestions and press Enter.
4. The bot shows upcoming raids. If none are planned, it will tell you.
5. Each entry shows the raid title, date and time, filled slots, and channel.
6. Click the channel, for example `#raids`, and find the matching raid post.
7. Check the title, date, and organizer's note. If everything looks good, follow [Join or leave a raid](./join-a-raid.md).

### What does `3 / 6` mean?

It shows the filled slots and the total group size. With `3 / 6`, three slots are still open.

### Can't find a raid that works for you?

You can start one yourself. See [How to create a raid](./create-a-raid.md).
