# Join or leave a raid

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/anmelden.md) · 🇬🇧 English

## Join
1. Click **Join raid** on the post.
2. Choose your character emote.
3. The bot privately confirms; your name appears on the post.

## Leave
Click **Leave raid** to free your slot. The organizer is fixed in Slot 1 and cannot free that slot this way.

Full raids do not have an automatic waiting list. Ask the organizer if a spot opens up.
