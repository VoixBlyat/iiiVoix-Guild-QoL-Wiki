# Our QoL bot: What you can do with it

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../bot-funktionen.md) · 🇬🇧 English

Welcome 👋 This page covers the features you can use as a member. Admin settings and admin commands are intentionally left out.

## Find and plan raids

### See upcoming raids

Use `/raid list` to get a private list of upcoming raids. It shows the title, date, available slots, and the channel containing the raid post.

[Find a raid step by step](./raids/find-a-raid.md)

### Start your own raid

Use `/raid create` to open a form. Choose the raid title, date, group size, and an optional short note for the group. The bot posts the raid and signs you up as the first participant.

For Rosso/Berthe CM, for example, you can choose **8 slots**. You already take Slot 1, so seven more people can join.

[How to create a raid](./raids/create-a-raid.md)

### How to use “Now”

- Want to start right away? Enter `Now` under **Date or Now** and leave **Start time** empty.
- Want to start later today? Enter `Now` and put a time such as `20:30` under **Start time**.
- For another day, enter a date. A time is required when you enter a date.

You can also enter a time range such as `20:30-21:00`. This means the raid starts at 20:30 and is planned to end at 21:00.

[More date and time examples](./raids/date-and-time.md)

## Join a raid

The raid post shows the start time, how many slots are filled, and who is coming.

- **Join raid:** Sign up, then choose your character emote.
- **Change character:** Change your emote without leaving the raid.
- **Leave raid:** Sign out and free your slot.
- **Options:** Opens a private menu with feedback. If you created the raid, you will also find tools for managing your own post.

[Understand the raid post](./raids/raid-post.md) · [Join or leave](./raids/join-a-raid.md) · [Change your emote](./raids/change-character-emote.md)

## Manage your own raid

If you created a raid, open **Options** to edit it. You can change the date, group size, or note, reserve a slot, remove someone from your raid, or delete the post.

When the raid is over, you can start a 30-minute timer. The post is removed automatically when the timer runs out. The timer does not start on its own.

## Feedback and bug reports

Have an idea, or did something break? Open **Options** in the raid post and choose **Send feedback**. Briefly describe what happened and what you expected.

A screenshot is optional, but it can make a bug easier to understand. When you send feedback, I receive your message privately by DM. To keep things from getting spammy, there is a five-minute cooldown per person.

[Send feedback with or without a screenshot](./raids/raid-post.md#report-a-bug-with-a-screenshot)

## CoboDex updates

When new CoboDex Babel updates come out, the bot can post them automatically in the server's update channel. You do not need to set anything up.

## If something does not work

Check the [FAQ](./faq.md) first. If you cannot find an answer, send me a short description using **Send feedback**. If the problem is about a specific raid, include the raid post link.

Got a GitHub account? You can also drop bugs, questions, or missing guides in an [issue](https://github.com/VoixBlyat/iiiVoix-Guild-QoL-Wiki/issues/new) :D
