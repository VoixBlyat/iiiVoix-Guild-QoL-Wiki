# Frequently asked questions

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../faq.md) · 🇬🇧 English

## How do I find raids?
Use `/raid list` for a private list. Click a channel to open its raid post. [Guide](./raids/find-a-raid.md)

## How do I join or leave?
In the raid post, click **Join raid** and choose an emote. Click **Leave raid** to sign out. [Guide](./raids/join-a-raid.md)

## What does `3 / 6` mean?
Three of six slots are filled; three are open.

## Is there a waiting list?
No. If a raid is full, ask the organizer or find another with `/raid list`.

## Can I change my emote later?
Yes. Use **Change character**; you stay signed up. [Guide](./raids/change-character-emote.md)

## Why did the raid post disappear?
The organizer can start a 30-minute deletion timer after the raid.

## How do I report a bug?
In the raid post, choose **Options → Send feedback** and describe the issue. A screenshot is optional. I receive your report privately by DM.
