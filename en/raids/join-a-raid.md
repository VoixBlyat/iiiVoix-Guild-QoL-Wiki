# Join a raid

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/anmelden.md) · 🇬🇧 English

Found a raid you want to join? Sign up using the buttons in the raid post.

## How to sign up

1. Read the raid title and date. Also check whether the organizer added a note.
2. Check the slots. For example, `3 / 6` means three people have joined and three slots are open.
3. Click **Join raid**.
4. The bot opens a small picker. Choose your character emote there.
5. Click the emote you want. The bot sends you a private confirmation.
6. Your name should now appear in the participant list on the raid post.

Only you can see the picker. The raid post and your sign-up are visible to server members.

## If the raid is full

At `6 / 6`, all slots are taken. The bot will not accept another sign-up. There is currently no automatic waiting list. Ask the organizer if a slot becomes available, or use `/raid list` to find another raid.

## If you can no longer make it

1. Open the raid post.
2. Click **Leave raid**.
3. The bot privately confirms that you have left.
4. Your name disappears from the list and your slot becomes available again.

The raid post's creator is automatically assigned Slot 1. That fixed slot cannot be freed with **Leave raid**.

## Change your character emote later

You do not need to leave the raid. Click **Change character** and choose another emote. Your sign-up stays in place. See [Change your character emote](./change-character-emote.md) for details.
