# Understand the raid post

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/raid-post.md) · 🇬🇧 English

The post shows the title, date, slots, participants, and an optional organizer note. `3 / 6` means three slots filled and three open.

- **Join raid:** Sign up and choose an emote.
- **Change character:** Change your emote without leaving.
- **Leave raid:** Sign out.
- **Options:** Private feedback menu; organizers also get controls for their own raid.

Your sign-up appears in the participant list and is visible to server members in the channel.

## Report a bug
Choose **Options → Send feedback** and describe the issue. You can attach a screenshot, but it is optional. I receive the report privately by DM.

After the raid, the organizer can start a 30-minute timer that deletes the post.
