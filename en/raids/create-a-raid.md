# Create a raid

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/erstellen.md) · 🇬🇧 English

1. In the text channel you want, run `/raid create` and select the bot command.
2. Fill in and submit the form. The bot posts the raid and puts you in Slot 1.

- **Raid title:** Choose the content, e.g. Rosso/Berth CM.
- **Date or Now:** Enter `Now` to start now, or a date such as `25.12.2026`.
- **Start time:** e.g. `20:30`; required with a date. A range like `20:30-21:00` is allowed.
- **Player slots:** Total group size (1–100), including you.
- **Optional message:** Short note for the group (up to 150 characters).

## Examples

**Start now, six-player Doom Aporia:** `Now`, leave time blank, set `6` slots. You are in; five slots remain.

**Eight-player Rosso/Berth CM:** Date `25.12.2026`, time `20:30`, `8` slots. You are Slot 1; seven more can join.

Manage your post later through **Options**. See [Dates and times](./date-and-time.md).
