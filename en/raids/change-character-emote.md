# Change your character emote

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/emote-aendern.md) · 🇬🇧 English

Click **Change character** on the raid post and choose another emote. You stay signed up.

If your emote is missing, choose **Enter manually** and enter its code. `:CeT:` is only an example. You must have joined the raid first.
