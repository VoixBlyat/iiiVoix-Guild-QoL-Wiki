# Find a raid

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/finden.md) · 🇬🇧 English

1. Type `/raid list` in Discord and select the Raid Manager command.
2. The private reply lists upcoming raids, dates, slots, and channels.
3. Click a channel to open its raid post.
4. Check the title, date, and organizer's note. To join, see [Join a raid](./join-a-raid.md).

`3 / 6` means three slots filled and three open. No suitable raid? [Create one](./create-a-raid.md).
