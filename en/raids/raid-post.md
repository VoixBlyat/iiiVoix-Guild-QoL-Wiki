# Understand the raid post

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/raid-post.md) · 🇬🇧 English

The raid post is the bot's message with all the information about a raid. It shows the date, who is coming, and buttons for joining or leaving.

## Information in the post

- **Title:** What the group is playing.
- **Start:** Date and time. If two times are shown, the first is the start and the second is the planned end.
- **Slots:** For example, `3 / 6`. The first number is how many slots are filled; the second is the total group size. With `3 / 6`, three slots are still open.
- **Note from the organizer:** Optional extra information, such as where the group will meet.
- **Participants:** The people who have joined. Their chosen character emote appears next to their name.

## The buttons

- **Join raid:** Sign up, then choose a character emote.
- **Change character:** Change your emote after signing up.
- **Leave raid:** Sign out and free your slot.
- **Options:** Opens a private menu. It includes **Send feedback**. If you created the raid, it also has organizer tools for your post.

## Report a bug with a screenshot

1. Click **Options**.
2. Choose **Send feedback** in the private menu.
3. Describe what happened and what you expected to happen.
4. If a picture helps, click **Screenshot (optional)** and upload one. The screenshot is optional, but the description is required.
5. Submit the report. I receive your message and image privately by DM.

Only you can see the pickers and bot replies. Your sign-up appears in the participant list and is visible to server members.

## Is there a deletion timer on the post?

The organizer started it, usually because the raid is over. The post shows when it will be deleted. Save any information you still need beforehand.
