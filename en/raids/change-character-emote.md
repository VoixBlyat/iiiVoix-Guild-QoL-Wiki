# Change your character emote

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/emote-aendern.md) · 🇬🇧 English

An emote next to your name in the raid post shows everyone which character you are bringing. You can change it at any time without losing your sign-up.

## How to change it

1. Find the raid post you have already joined.
2. Click **Change character**.
3. A small picker opens. Only you can see it.
4. Open the list and choose your emote.
5. The bot confirms the change. The new emote now appears next to your name in the raid post.

## Your emote is not in the list?

1. Click **Enter manually** in the picker.
2. An input box opens. Enter your emote code there.
3. Check the spelling and uppercase/lowercase letters carefully.
4. Submit the form.

`:CeT:` is only an example and probably is not your emote. Enter your own emote code. If you do not know it, ask someone on the server.

## Haven't joined yet?

**Change character** only works after you have joined. Click **Join raid** first, choose your emote, and confirm your sign-up.
