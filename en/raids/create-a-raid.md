# Create a raid

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/erstellen.md) · 🇬🇧 English

Want to start a group yourself? Use `/raid create` to make a raid post. You do not need any special settings.

## Step by step

1. Go to the text channel where you want the raid post to appear.
2. Type `/raid create`.
3. Select **raid create** from the bot's suggestions. A form opens.
4. Fill in the fields. Each one is explained below.
5. Submit the form.
6. The bot posts the raid in that channel and pings the raid role so the group sees it.
7. You are automatically signed up in **Slot 1**. Choose your character emote afterward.

## What goes in each field?

- **Raid title:** Choose what you want to play, such as Doom Aporia, Serpentium, or Rosso/Berth CM.
- **Date or Now:** Enter `Now` if you want to start right away. For a later date, enter a date such as `25.12` or `25.12.2026`.
- **Start time:** Enter a time such as `20:30`. A time is required when you enter a date. You can also enter a start and end time: `20:30-21:00`.
- **Player slots:** How many people can join in total. The allowed range is 1 to 100. If you enter `6`, there are six slots total—including your own.
- **Optional message:** Add a short note for the group, such as “Please wait in voice beforehand.” This is optional and can be up to 150 characters.

## Complete example: start right away

You want to start a six-person Doom Aporia raid now:

- **Raid title:** Doom Aporia
- **Date or Now:** `Now`
- **Start time:** leave empty
- **Player slots:** `6`
- **Optional message:** `Please wait in voice beforehand`

The bot posts the raid right away. You are already in Slot 1, so five slots are still open.

## Complete example: eight-person Rosso/Berth CM

Your Rosso/Berth CM group needs eight people in total. You plan to start on December 25, 2026, at 20:30:

- **Raid title:** Rosso/Berth CM
- **Date or Now:** `25.12.2026`
- **Start time:** `20:30`
- **Player slots:** `8`
- **Optional message:** `Please wait in voice beforehand`

The bot creates eight slots. You are already in Slot 1, so seven more people can join.

More examples are in [Enter a date and time](./date-and-time.md).

## Can I edit the post later?

Yes. If you created the post, open **Options** to find the organizer tools for your raid.
