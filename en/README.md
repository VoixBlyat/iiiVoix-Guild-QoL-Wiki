Welcome 👋

This wiki explains how to use the raid features on our Discord server. The guides are written for members and leave out admin settings.

## A few useful words

- **Raid:** A group of players taking on the same content together.
- **Raid post:** The bot's post with the date, available slots, and participants.
- **Slot:** One place in the group. `3 / 6` means 3 places are filled and 3 are still open.
- **Organizer:** The person who created the raid post.
- **Character emote:** The emote next to your name that shows which character you are bringing.
