# Frequently asked questions

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../faq.md) · 🇬🇧 English

## What is a raid post?

It is the bot's message with the raid title, date, available slots, participants, and buttons for joining or leaving. The [raid post guide](./raids/raid-post.md) explains each part.

## Where can I find upcoming raids?

Type `/raid list`. The bot sends you a private list. Each entry shows the date, slots, and channel. Click the channel and find the raid post there.

## How do I join a raid?

Open the raid post, click **Join raid**, and choose your character emote. Your name will then appear in the participant list.

## What does `3 / 6` mean?

Three people have joined, and there are six slots in total. That means three slots are still available.

## Can I leave a raid?

Yes. Click **Leave raid** in the raid post. Your slot becomes available again. The organizer is fixed in Slot 1 and cannot free that slot this way.

## Can I change my emote?

Yes. Click **Change character** and choose another emote. You stay signed up.

## The raid is full. Is there a waiting list?

No, there is no automatic waiting list. Ask the organizer if a slot becomes available, or use `/raid list` to look for another raid.

## Why can't I find `/raid list`?

Make sure you are in our Discord server and select the command from the Raid Manager bot. If it still does not show up, ask the server moderators.

## Why did my raid post disappear?

If the post showed a deletion timer, the organizer started it. The post is removed when the timer runs out. Save any information you still need beforehand.

## How do I report a bug with a screenshot?

Open **Options** in the raid post and choose **Send feedback**. Describe what happened in the text box. If a picture would help, upload one under **Screenshot (optional)**. The screenshot is optional, but the description is required. I receive your report privately by DM.
