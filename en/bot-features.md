# Our QoL bot: Quick guide

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../bot-funktionen.md) · 🇬🇧 English

Main member features (admin settings are not covered here):

- **Browse raids:** `/raid list` privately shows upcoming raids and their channels.
- **Create a raid:** `/raid create` opens a form. The organizer is automatically in Slot 1.
- **Join or leave:** Use **Join raid**, **Change character**, or **Leave raid** on the post.
- **Manage your raid:** Under **Options**, edit it, reserve slots, remove participants, or delete the post. After the raid, you can start a 30-minute deletion timer.
- **Send feedback:** **Options → Send feedback**. A screenshot is optional; I receive reports privately by DM.
- **CoboDex:** New Babel updates are posted automatically in the designated channel.

For Rosso/Berthe CM, set **8 slots** total: you take Slot 1 and seven more players can join.

**Date:** Enter `Now` and leave the time blank to start right away. For later today, enter `Now` plus a time such as `20:30`. For another day, enter a date and time. [Examples](./raids/date-and-time.md)
