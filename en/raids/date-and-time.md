# Dates and times

[🇩🇪 Deutsch](../../raids/zeiten.md) · 🇬🇧 English

The form has **Date or Now** and **Start time**:

- **Start now:** Enter `Now`; leave the time blank.
- **Later today:** Enter `Now` and a future time, e.g. `20:30`.
- **Another day:** Enter a date as `DD.MM` or `DD.MM.YYYY` plus a time, e.g. `25.12.2026` and `20:30`.
- **Time range:** `20:30-21:00` means start at 20:30, planned end at 21:00—not “sometime between”.

Use dots and 24-hour time. Past dates/times and a date without a time are rejected. If you leave out the year, the bot chooses the next matching date.
